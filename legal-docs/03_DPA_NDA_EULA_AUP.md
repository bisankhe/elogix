# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (DPA)
## NepHR Pro — GDPR / UK GDPR / DPDP Compliant
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** June 11, 2026

---

## PARTIES

**Data Processor:** NepHR Pro Pvt. Ltd. ("Processor")
**Data Controller:** [Client Organization Name] ("Controller")

This DPA forms part of the main Terms of Service agreement and governs the processing of personal data.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

- **"Personal Data"** — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person
- **"Processing"** — any operation performed on personal data
- **"Data Subject"** — the individual whose personal data is processed (employees, candidates)
- **"Sub-Processor"** — third party engaged by NepHR Pro to process data

---

## 2. SUBJECT MATTER & PURPOSE

The Processor shall process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller solely for:
- Operating the NepHR Pro HRIS/HRMS/HRIM platform
- Providing HR management services as described in the Terms of Service
- Complying with legal obligations applicable to the Processor
- No other purpose without prior written instruction from the Controller

---

## 3. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECTS & DATA

**Data Subjects:** Employees, contractors, job candidates, emergency contacts

**Categories of Personal Data:**
- Identity data (name, ID numbers, date of birth)
- Contact data (address, email, phone)
- Financial data (salary, bank details, tax information)
- Employment data (job title, department, performance)
- Attendance and leave data
- Sensitive data (health records, biometrics — only where legally permitted)

---

## 4. PROCESSOR OBLIGATIONS

The Processor shall:

**4.1 Instructions** — Process data only on documented instructions from the Controller

**4.2 Confidentiality** — Ensure all authorized personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations

**4.3 Security** — Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:
- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.3 encryption in transit
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Multi-tenant data isolation
- Regular security testing (annual VAPT)
- ISO/IEC 27001-aligned ISMS

**4.4 Sub-processors** — Only engage sub-processors listed in Schedule A with adequate protections

**4.5 Data Subject Rights** — Assist Controller in responding to data subject requests

**4.6 Security Incidents** — Notify Controller within **72 hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach

**4.7 Data Protection Impact Assessments** — Assist Controller in conducting DPIAs

**4.8 Deletion** — Delete or return all personal data upon termination (within 90 days)

**4.9 Audits** — Allow for and contribute to audits conducted by the Controller or its auditor

---

## 5. CONTROLLER OBLIGATIONS

The Controller shall:
- Ensure lawful basis for all data processing instructions
- Ensure data subjects are informed of processing
- Comply with all applicable data protection laws
- Obtain necessary consents for special category data
- Only instruct processing that is lawful and proportionate

---

## 6. SUB-PROCESSORS (SCHEDULE A)

| Sub-Processor | Purpose | Location | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services | Cloud hosting | Region-specific | SCCs / DPA |
| Anthropic (Claude API) | AI assistant | USA | SCCs + DPA |
| Payment processor | Billing | Region-specific | PCI DSS + DPA |
| Email service provider | Notifications | Region-specific | DPA |

Controller will be notified 30 days before adding new sub-processors.
Controller may object to new sub-processors in writing within 14 days.

---

## 7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

For transfers outside the EEA/UK:
- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** — EU Commission approved
- **International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTA)** — UK ICO approved
- **Adequacy decisions** where applicable

Data residency options available per Section 11 of Privacy Policy.

---

## 8. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

On discovery of a personal data breach, Processor will:
1. Notify Controller within **72 hours** (GDPR requirement)
2. Provide: nature of breach, categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, likely consequences, measures taken
3. Cooperate fully with Controller's incident response
4. Assist Controller in notifying supervisory authorities and data subjects

---

## 9. TERM & TERMINATION

This DPA is effective for the duration of the main service agreement.
On termination, Processor will delete all personal data within 90 days.
Processor will provide written certification of deletion upon request.

---

## 10. GOVERNING LAW

This DPA is governed by the same law as the main Terms of Service agreement, unless local law requires otherwise (e.g., UK GDPR requires English law for UK transfers).

# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)
## NepHR Pro — Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** Upon Signing

---

## PARTIES

**Party A:** NepHR Pro Pvt. Ltd. ("NepHR Pro")
**Party B:** [Client/Partner Organization Name] ("Recipient")

Collectively referred to as "the Parties"

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship involving NepHR Pro's HRIS/HRMS/HRIM platform and related services. In connection with this, each Party may disclose confidential information to the other.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party, including but not limited to:
- Software architecture, source code, and algorithms
- AI model details, training data, and accuracy metrics
- Business plans, pricing, and financial data
- Customer lists and employee data
- Security configurations and vulnerability reports
- VAPT reports and audit findings
- HR data of any organization's employees
- Technical documentation and roadmaps
- Any information marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary"

---

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

Each Party agrees to:
- Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
- Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent
- Use only for the stated business purpose
- Apply at least the same level of protection as its own confidential information (minimum: reasonable care)
- Notify the other Party immediately upon discovering any unauthorized disclosure

---

## 4. EXCEPTIONS

Confidentiality obligations do NOT apply to information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient
- Was already known to the Recipient before disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law, court order, or regulatory authority (with prior written notice where permitted)

---

## 5. EMPLOYEE HR DATA — SPECIAL PROTECTION

Any employee personal data shared during evaluation or implementation:
- Must be handled under a full Data Processing Agreement
- Must comply with Nepal Privacy Act 2018, GDPR, DPDP Act, and other applicable laws
- Must be returned or destroyed within 30 days of agreement termination
- May NOT be used for any purpose other than agreed evaluation

---

## 6. TERM

This NDA is effective from the date of signing and continues for:
- **3 years** from the date of signing, OR
- **5 years** for HR/employee personal data specifically

---

## 7. RETURN OF INFORMATION

Upon request or termination, each Party shall:
- Return all Confidential Information and copies
- Certify in writing that all copies have been destroyed
- Delete all digital copies within 30 days

---

## 8. REMEDIES

Breach of this NDA may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are insufficient. Each Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to other legal remedies.

---

## 9. GOVERNING LAW

This NDA shall be governed by the laws of Nepal (or as mutually agreed for international parties).

# ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY (AUP)
## NepHR Pro Platform
**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** June 11, 2026

---

## PURPOSE

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the rules for responsible and lawful use of the NepHR Pro platform. All users must comply.

---

## PERMITTED USE

✅ Managing employee records and HR operations
✅ Processing payroll, leave, and attendance
✅ Running legitimate recruitment processes
✅ Generating HR reports and analytics
✅ Using AI features for advisory HR insights
✅ Communicating with employees through built-in modules

---

## PROHIBITED USE

### 🚫 Privacy Violations
- Collecting data beyond what is necessary for HR purposes
- Accessing employee data without legitimate HR need
- Sharing employee PII with unauthorized third parties
- Using employee data for purposes not disclosed to them
- Processing sensitive data (health, biometrics) without consent

### 🚫 Discrimination & Harassment
- Using AI features to discriminate based on protected characteristics (gender, race, religion, disability, age, nationality)
- Processing data to identify employees' union membership
- Using performance data to target employees for protected activity
- Any form of employee surveillance beyond lawful monitoring

### 🚫 Security Violations
- Attempting to access other tenants' data
- Sharing login credentials between users
- Attempting to bypass RBAC or security controls
- Running unauthorized security scans or penetration tests
- Uploading malware, scripts, or malicious content

### 🚫 Legal Violations
- Processing data in violation of Nepal Labour Act 2017
- Violating GDPR, UK GDPR, DPDP Act, or other applicable privacy laws
- Using the platform to facilitate illegal employment practices
- Retaining employee data beyond legally permitted periods
- Failing to honor employee rights (erasure, portability, access)

### 🚫 Technical Abuse
- Automated scraping or bulk data extraction without authorization
- Overloading servers with excessive API calls
- Attempting to reverse engineer the platform
- Using the platform to mine cryptocurrency

---

## HR DATA STANDARDS

All HR data entered must be:
- **Accurate** — factually correct and up to date
- **Relevant** — necessary for legitimate HR purposes
- **Lawful** — collected with proper consent or legal basis
- **Secure** — handled by authorized personnel only

---

## AI USE STANDARDS

When using AI features:
- AI recommendations are **advisory only** — human decision required
- Document human review of all AI-influenced employment decisions
- Regularly audit AI outputs for bias or inaccuracies
- Report AI anomalies to support@nephrpro.com

---

## REPORTING VIOLATIONS

Report AUP violations to: security@nephrpro.com
All reports are investigated confidentially.
Violations may result in immediate account suspension.

---

## ENFORCEMENT

Violations may result in:
1. Warning and required training
2. Temporary account suspension
3. Permanent termination without refund
4. Legal action where applicable
