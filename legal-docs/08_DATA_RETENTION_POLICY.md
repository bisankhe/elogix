## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Data Retention Policy defines:
- How long eLogix HRMS retains each category of personal data
- The legal basis for each retention period
- Deletion and destruction procedures
- Country-specific legal requirements

**Applies to:** All personal data processed through eLogix HRMS across all tenants and all jurisdictions.

---

## 2. GUIDING PRINCIPLES

| Principle | Description |
|---|---|
| **Minimization** | Retain only what is legally required |
| **Purpose limitation** | Data kept only for its original purpose |
| **Accuracy** | Outdated data is updated or deleted |
| **Security** | Retained data is protected throughout lifecycle |
| **Accountability** | Deletion is documented and certified |

## 5. DELETION PROCEDURES

### 5.1 Standard Deletion Process
```
Step 1: Retention expiry flagged by automated system
Step 2: Legal review — confirm no active obligation to retain
Step 3: Soft delete — marked as deleted, hidden from UI (30 days)
Step 4: Hard delete — permanently removed from primary database
Step 5: Backup purge — removed from all backup systems (90 days)
Step 6: Certificate issued — written confirmation of deletion
```

### 5.2 Right to Erasure (GDPR/UK GDPR) Process
```
Step 1: Erasure request received
Step 2: Acknowledge within 72 hours
Step 3: Legal team verifies — no overriding retention obligation
Step 4: Complete deletion within 30 days
Step 5: Notify requestor of completion
Step 6: Log in erasure register
```

### 5.3 Secure Deletion Standards
- **Digital data:** NIST SP 800-88 secure erasure standard
- **Database records:** Cryptographic erasure + overwrite
- **Backups:** Removed from all backup rotations within 90 days
- **Physical media:** DoD 5220.22-M destruction standard

## 7. EXCEPTIONS TO RETENTION LIMITS

Data may be retained beyond standard periods when:
- Subject to active legal proceedings or regulatory investigation
- Required by court order or government authority
- Covered by a litigation hold notice
- Required for ongoing contractual obligations

All exceptions must be documented and approved by the DPO.
